Part B — Reduced relative clauses

The rule in three lines
  • Passive reduction: who / which / that + be disappears and the past participle stays → the homework which was set on Monday → the homework set on Monday.
  • Active reduction: the pronoun disappears and the verb becomes -ing → people who live in cities → people living in cities.
  • No reduction when the pronoun is the object (the book which I bought) or when you have whose.
