How I work

I've worked with language models since 2022 — prompting them, testing their reasoning, watching where they fail.

I build with them on one condition: they extend human judgment, they don't stand in for it. Bias, gaps, and confident errors are the interesting part — the places where a philosopher's training earns its keep. That criterion is the whole point.
