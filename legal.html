Privacy

  • This site has no accounts, no logins, no forms and no cookies, so it shows no cookie banner.
  • The pages do not run analytics or advertising scripts.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts and icons load from unpkg. Those services see your IP address when your browser requests the files, as with any hosted font or icon library.
  • The language switch on the philosophy page saves your choice in your own browser (local storage). It never leaves your device.
  • Classroom pieces linked from here check answers inside the browser. A result is sent to a teacher only when a student presses the send button.
  • The role playing game Terra and the pages hosted by GitHub follow their own hosts' practices.
