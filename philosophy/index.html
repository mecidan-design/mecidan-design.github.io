Teaching tools · classroom-tested

Philosophy, made walkable

Material for fifth-year secondary philosophy in Buenos Aires. The through-line is one idea: an argument is something a student should have to use, not something to memorise. So the same text comes back in four forms — read, simulated, argued, played. Everything is Spanish-language, self-contained HTML, and runs in a school browser with no accounts and no student data.

The unit · five lessons

Descartes — Meditaciones metafísicas

Five scrollytelling lessons that follow a single staircase: doubt goes all the way down demolishing everything, and then knowledge climbs back up recovering what fell. Doubt and the evil demon, the cogito and the wax, the proofs of God, the origin of error, and the return of the world. Start here — the pieces below hang off this unit.
